# 🌍 HR Process Optimization at Synergy Enterprises (SAP SuccessFactors)

## 🏢 Company Background
Synergy Enterprises is a multinational FMCG (Fast-Moving Consumer Goods) manufacturer operating across five continents with over 75,000 employees globally. The company manages a large, diverse workforce and requires efficient, standardized HR processes to support global operations.

As Synergy Enterprises expanded internationally, managing HR functions across regions became increasingly complex. Different countries used separate systems for payroll, recruitment, and performance management, creating inefficiencies and compliance risks.

---

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focused on optimizing HR processes at Synergy Enterprises by proposing the implementation of **SAP SuccessFactors**, a unified cloud-based HR platform.

The goal was to centralize HR operations, streamline global processes, ensure regulatory compliance, and improve employee experience.

---

## ❗ Problem Statement
Synergy Enterprises used multiple disconnected HR systems across regions, resulting in:
- Fragmented employee data  
- Manual and time-consuming processes  
- Limited visibility into global HR metrics  
- High IT maintenance costs  
- Compliance risks with data protection and labour laws  

These challenges limited HR’s ability to support business strategy and impacted employee satisfaction.

---

## 💡 Proposed Solution
Implement **SAP SuccessFactors Global HR Suite** to integrate:
- Core HR  
- Payroll  
- Recruitment  
- Performance Management  
- Learning & Development  

All under one centralized platform.

---
